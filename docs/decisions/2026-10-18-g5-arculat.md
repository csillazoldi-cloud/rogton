## Why light mode uses ink as the primary colour

Neon on white is 1.2:1 — unreadable as text — and `--primary` is used both as a fill (13 rules) and as a text colour (24 rules). In
light mode the primary is therefore ink with neon text on it (17:1); neon stays the fill colour in dark mode (ink text, 17:1).
Orange is a fill with ink text in both modes (`--on-accent`, 5.8:1); orange as TEXT on white is the darker #B83200 (6.0:1).
